General Questions

Stackly is an enterprise-grade AI platform that provides developers and businesses access to state-of-the-art Large Language Models, Vision generators, and Audio transcribers via a unified, low-latency API.

Our Developer (Free) tier gives you access to our base Stackly-LLM-Turbo text model with a limit of 100 requests per month. Vision, Audio, and advanced LLMs require a Professional subscription.

Click on the "Sign Up" button on the top right corner of the page. You can register using your email address, or via Single Sign-On (SSO) with Google or GitHub.

Plans & Billing

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts for the Pro plan. You can cancel your subscription from the Billing settings page at any time, and you will not be billed for the following month. You will retain access until the end of your current billing cycle.

If you are on the Developer tier, your requests will be rejected with a 429 Too Many Requests error until the next month. On the Pro tier, you can configure "Overage Billing" to automatically pay for extra requests, or hard-cap it to prevent unexpected charges.

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for your first month on the Pro plan. If you are not satisfied, contact support within 14 days of your initial payment for a full refund.
